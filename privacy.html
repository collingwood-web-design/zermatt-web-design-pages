Zermatt Web Design (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through www.zermattwebdesign.com, how we use it, and the choices you have. We operate in Collingwood, Ontario, Canada, with no Swiss office.

We have aligned this privacy policy primarily with Swiss data protection law (FADP/nDSG) and applicable Canadian privacy law (including PIPEDA). Certain types of processing may also be subject to the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). In those cases, this policy includes relevant notes. Whether the GDPR applies can only be assessed on a case-by-case basis. If you have questions, please contact us.

Information we collect

We collect personal information that you choose to give us, and limited technical information created when you use the site (for example IP address, browser/device details, pages viewed and timestamps held by hosting providers).

Contact form. If you submit our contact form, we collect the details you enter, which may include your name, email address, phone number, subject and message. Please do not send highly sensitive data, payment-card details or identity documents through the form.

Email and phone. If you contact us directly, we keep the information you share so we can respond and, if you ask, discuss or deliver our services.

Spam protection. The contact form includes a hidden honeypot field used only to help block automated spam. Legitimate visitors leave that field blank.

How we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and schedule conversations
  • Provide quotes, project work and ongoing support you request
  • Operate, secure and improve this website
  • Meet legal or professional obligations when required

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use contact-form submissions for third-party advertising or build marketing profiles from site visits.

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on the applicable legal bases in Article 6 GDPR — in particular Art. 6(1)(b) (steps prior to or performance of a contract), Art. 6(1)(f) (legitimate interests such as securing and improving the website), Art. 6(1)(c) (legal obligation where required), and Art. 6(1)(a) (consent, if we ask for it). You may withdraw consent for the future where processing is based on consent.

How we share information

We share personal information only when needed to run our business or when the law requires it:

  • Website hosting. Static site delivery (including GitHub Pages).
  • Media CDN. Images and media via Cloudflare (media.cwd-cdn.com).
  • Form and email delivery. Contact-form messages are sent through our form and email delivery service (CWD contact service on Railway; email delivery via Resend) so your inquiry reaches our team.
  • Reviews. Our homepage may embed Google reviews through Trustindex, which may collect technical data under its own policies.
  • Invoicing. Project invoices in CHF may be sent through Helcim or QuickBooks.
  • Legal requests. We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect our rights, safety or property.

Fonts are hosted locally on this website (not Google Fonts).

International transfers

Because we are based in Canada, personal information may be processed in Canada. Depending on our service providers, data may also be processed in or accessed from the United States or the European Union. Where Swiss law requires an adequacy decision or other transfer safeguard for a destination country, we rely on the applicable legal basis.

Cookies and similar technologies

This site does not set its own advertising or analytics cookies. Third-party embeds (for example Trustindex) may set or read cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Retention

We keep inquiry and project-related communications for as long as needed to respond, deliver services, keep business records and meet legal requirements. When information is no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it when practical.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to a small design studio, including HTTPS and limited account access. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.
